# User Feedback — KiloCarrier

- **How collected:** Written website review from one user, provided as a feedback summary image
- **When:** July 25, 2026
- **Website reviewed:** [kilocarrier.com](https://kilocarrier.com/)
- **Original feedback:** [KiloCarrier feedback summary](./kilocarrier_feedback_summary_page-0001.jpg)

## Raw feedback

1. **Strengths:** The reviewer found the value proposition and “Travel light. Send smart.” message clear. They liked the zero platform fees, direct contact through Telegram, WhatsApp, Viber, and Facebook, and the clean UI/UX with an accessible FAQ.
2. **Areas for improvement:** Add a “Notify Me” route alert or Telegram group link when there are no matching trips; add an English/Myanmar language switcher; strengthen trust and safety with a safety-guidelines checklist and carrier-verification badge; and make travel-date and available-weight filters easier to use.
3. **Summary:** The reviewer considered KiloCarrier’s core concept and landing-page structure strong. Their main concern is that the platform needs enough active carriers and senders—balanced supply and demand—to remain useful.

## Themes (what keeps coming up)

- The core idea is easy to understand, and direct contact with zero platform fees is attractive.
- Localization and clearer filtering would make the service easier for its target users.
- Trust signals are important for a peer-to-peer cargo marketplace.
- Empty states should help users stay engaged while the marketplace is building supply.

## Top 3 things to fix

- [ ] Add an English/Myanmar language switcher.
- [ ] Add a safety-guidelines checklist and a carrier-verification badge.
- [ ] Add a “Notify Me” route alert or Telegram group link to empty trip results.
